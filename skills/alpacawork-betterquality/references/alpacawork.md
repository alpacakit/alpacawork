# Asking a fresh AI in alpacawork

- **Who looks.** A reviewer or a builder of an alternative is a member of your task (`agent assign`, then `task request --agent`), or the conversation's own subagent for a short, one-off look. An audience stand-in is usually the conversation's own subagent, because it must be new each round and must not inherit your conversation; place it as a member only when the stand-in needs tools a subagent lacks, and then start each round with `--new-conversation`. Choose the agent, model and effort as [alpacawork's request procedure](../../alpacawork/references/request.md) says; the values you can choose are the `params` of agents in `alpacawork config get`.
- **One request.** Give a reviewer everything in one request: the user's original words and what was agreed afterwards, the frame as a read command (when it is already written in the request text or the task folder), the real thing at the version under review with the startup and cleanup the review needs, what you already verified and how, and a pointer to [reviewer.md](reviewer.md) instead of a copy of it. The re-review goes to the same member in the same conversation.
- **Handing the building to a new agent.** When the builder is a member or a child's owner, place another agent, or send the next request with `--new-conversation`. When the builder is you, hand the building to a child and keep the judging; starting your own next conversation anew instead (`--next-conversation new`, on a report that carries the values) works only when your turn ends on a question or an open request — a `doing` report alone does not let you end the turn. Pass the user's words and the frame, the verified facts, the decisions and approvals that remain, and the approach to drop with the reasons (as a read command); name the parts worth reusing, and do not pass the old result as the thing to continue from. The commands and conditions follow [Tasks and requests: the common procedure](../../alpacawork/references/request.md) and ["Reports" in Owner](../../alpacawork/references/owner.md#reports).
- **Records.** Use `--taken adopted` when you take a reviewer's verdict, including when it asks you to fix the work. Use `fix` or `redo` if the review itself needs more work. A reviewer called as the conversation's own subagent leaves no Run: put its verdict and required findings in the request for the fix or in the report. Records, completion and acceptance follow ["Checking a result against the request"](../../alpacawork/SKILL.md#checking-a-result-against-the-request) and ["Delivery and completion"](../../alpacawork/references/lifecycle.md).
